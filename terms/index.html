TruckHive Terms of Use

Version 2026-11-13

TruckHive is a community app where professional drivers rate shippers, receivers and freight brokers and share short videos. By creating an account you agree to these Terms.

Who can use TruckHive

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • One account per person. Keep your password private; you are responsible for activity on your account.

What you can post

Reviews, comments, videos, photos and profile details are your content. You keep ownership and give TruckHive a license to host, display and distribute it inside the app for as long as it is posted.

Reviews must describe your own, first-hand experience with that shipper, receiver or broker.

What is not allowed

TruckHive has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not post:

  • Harassment, threats, bullying or hate based on race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or national origin.
  • Sexual content or nudity.
  • Graphic violence or gore. Non-graphic road incidents are allowed; graphic footage is removed.
  • Names, photos or personal details of individual employees, dispatchers or other private people.
  • False statements of fact you know are untrue, spam, or reviews you were paid or pressured to write.
  • Anything illegal, or anything recorded while you were driving.

We screen posts automatically and review reports from drivers within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these Terms and may suspend or close the accounts that post it.

Report and block

Every video, comment, review and profile has Report and Block options. Blocking hides that driver’s content from you and yours from them.

Your handle on reviews

Reviews show your handle, the name you picked. They never show your real name or email. Reviews posted anonymously before November 13, 2026 stay anonymous. TruckHive always knows who posted a review, and we may disclose account information when required by law or valid legal process.

Review badges

A badge shows how a review was checked. "Verified at facility" means the driver’s phone was at the facility, checked by GPS. "Timed visit" means the driver timed their wait there with the TruckHive wait timer. A badge shows the driver was there. It does not mean TruckHive agrees with or vouches for what the review says.

Safety

Never use TruckHive while driving. Federal rules (49 CFR 392.82) prohibit hand-held phone use while operating a commercial motor vehicle. The app locks itself while the vehicle is moving.

Subscriptions

Ad-free ($1.99/month) and OG Truckers ($19.99/month) are auto-renewing subscriptions billed by Apple or Google. They renew each month unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Prices may vary by region.

Videos expire

Videos delete themselves automatically: after 24 hours on Free, 5 days on Ad-free and 10 days on OG Truckers, based on your plan when you posted.

Ending your account

You can delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete account. We may suspend or close accounts that break these Terms.

No warranty

Ratings are opinions of individual drivers. TruckHive checks where reviews were posted from (see Review badges) but does not verify what they say, and is not responsible for decisions you make based on them. The app is provided "as is" to the extent allowed by law.
