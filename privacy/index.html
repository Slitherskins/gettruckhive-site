TruckHive Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-04

What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (stored hashed by our auth provider), handle, optional photo, bio and carrier name.
  • What you post: reviews, comments, videos, photos, and the shippers you add.
  • Settings: your privacy and notification choices.
  • Device: push notification token, app version, and — on the free plan — the advertising identifier if you allow tracking.
  • Location: used on your phone for the driving lock, to find shippers near you, to confirm you’re at a facility you rate, and to run the wait timer. Your coordinates are sent only to look up nearby facilities or to check that you’re at the facility, and are not stored: a rating keeps only whether you were there (yes/no), and a timed wait keeps only its start and end times, the visit type and that same yes/no. Location is optional.
  • Motion: if you deny location, the phone’s motion activity ("in a vehicle") is used for the driving lock, on your phone only.

What other drivers see

Your handle, photo, bio and videos. Never your email, never your list of reviews. Anonymous reviews show "Verified driver" instead of your handle. Your carrier name only shows if you turn that on. We never attach your city or GPS location to your videos.

How we use it

To run the app, screen content for safety, answer reports, send the alerts you turned on, and show ads to free users.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — database, file storage and sign-in (hosted in the United States).
  • OpenAI — automated screening of text and images.
  • Sightengine — automated screening of videos.
  • Google AdMob — ads for free users.
  • Apple and Google — subscriptions and push notifications.
  • FMCSA — broker lookups use only the MC, DOT number or name you search for.

We do not sell your personal information.

Location and video metadata

GPS data is removed from every video before it is stored.

How long we keep it

Videos delete automatically when they expire. Other content stays until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account removes your profile and content.

Your choices

Change privacy and notification settings in the app. Delete your account in Settings → Account, or request deletion by emailing support@gettruckhive.com.

Children

TruckHive is for adults 18 and older.
